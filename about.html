ABOUT GROUND TO SUPERSTRUCTURE

Independent by approach.
Procurement-led by design.

Ground to Superstructure (GTS) is an independent sourcing and supply enterprise. We help businesses investigate options beyond their usual supply routes and coordinate an appropriate response around each requirement.

Discuss a project ↗Explore our approach ↗

OUR ROLE

A sourcing partner,
built around your brief.

Every sourcing conversation starts with understanding what is needed: the product or service, specification, volume, location, programme and any commercial considerations. GTS researches potential supply channels, identifies suitable market participants and assesses options relevant to the brief.

Where appropriate, GTS can explore commercial terms and coordinate agreed supply arrangements through third-party manufacturers, distributors and specialist suppliers. The scope and next steps are considered individually with the customer.

A CLEAR BUSINESS MODEL

We coordinate supply.
We don’t claim to own it.

GTS operates as a procurement-led sourcing enterprise. We do not present ourselves as a construction contractor or as the owner of stock, warehouses, factories, vehicles or transport infrastructure. Supply and delivery arrangements depend on appropriate third-party channels and the agreed requirement.
