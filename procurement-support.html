STRUCTURED SOURCING TIMELINE

Clear milestones.
Progress with purpose.

For assignments that benefit from a staged market review, agreed milestones can help keep sourcing activity and reporting clear.

  1. 01

    DAY 0

    The Brief

    Confirm the specification, quantity, compliance requirements, budget or target where available, delivery programme and location, then begin market mapping.

  2. 02

    DAY 30

    Progress Update

    Review market response, supplier discussions, potential solutions and identified constraints.

  3. 03

    DAY 60

    Progress Update

    Review shortlisted sourcing routes, capacity, indicative commercial position, lead times and technical requirements.

  4. 04

    DAY 90

    Commercial Proposal

    Where applicable, consider a quotation, samples, technical documentation and a proposed supply programme.

This timeline is illustrative. Complex assignments may require a longer timeframe, to be agreed with the client. Progress depends on the specification, supplier response, technical assessment and commercial discussions.

DEFINED SOURCING MANDATE

Clear scope.
Agreed expectations.

For complex, bespoke, high-volume or specialist sourcing assignments, GTS may agree a defined sourcing mandate with the client establishing the requirement, sourcing period, reporting milestones and commercial parameters before work commences.

Any exclusivity, sourcing fees, confidentiality or non-circumvention arrangements would subsequently be agreed contractually for an individual assignment where appropriate.

Exclusivity is not a standard requirement and applies only if separately agreed for an assignment.
