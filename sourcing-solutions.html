A CLEAR WORKING FRAMEWORK

A defined
sourcing mandate.

Before work begins, the parties can agree an assignment-specific mandate so that scope, expectations and commercial arrangements are clear.

The agreed mandate may set out:

  • Assignment scopeThe requirement, deliverables and boundaries of the work.
  • Sourcing periodThe intended duration and any review points.
  • Reporting milestonesProgress updates and communication expectations.
  • Commercial parametersBudget assumptions, pricing basis and decision process.
  • ConfidentialityHow sensitive project and supplier information is to be handled.
  • Potential feesAny fees or other charges, agreed before the assignment proceeds.
  • Non-circumventionAny applicable terms, if agreed in writing for the assignment.
  • ExclusivityOnly where separately and expressly agreed; it is not automatic.

  2. 02

    DAY 30

  3. 03

    DAY 60

    Progress Update

    Review supplier feedback, open questions, capacity and the sourcing route under consideration.

  4. 04

    DAY 90

    Commercial Proposal

    Where practicable, present a proposal for consideration based on findings and supplier response.

Timing depends on: specification, supplier availability, manufacturing requirements, capacity, technical assessment, commercial negotiation and delivery programme.

Milestones are illustrative and subject to the agreed mandate, complexity and supply-market conditions. A commercial proposal is not guaranteed.
